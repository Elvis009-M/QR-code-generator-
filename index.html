Frequently asked questions

Is this QR code generator free?

Yes. It is free, has no sign-up and no limit on how many codes you make.

Do my QR codes expire?

No. These are static QR codes. The data is stored in the code itself, so it works forever.

Is my data stored anywhere?

Links and text are never sent to a server; codes are generated in your browser. If you use the Image option, your image is uploaded to Cloudinary and is public to anyone with the QR code.

Which error correction level should I choose?

Use Medium for everyday codes. Choose High if you print on rough surfaces or plan to place a logo over the center.
